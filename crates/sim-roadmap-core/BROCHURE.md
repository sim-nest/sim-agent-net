# Roadmaps that say exactly what they mean

`sim-roadmap-core` turns authored intent into small, immutable values. Each
revision pins its imports, bounds its size, names its acceptance obligations,
and gives implementers reviewed guidance tied to exact source evidence.

The crate deliberately stops at meaning. Sketches are useful guidance, never
proof; acceptance projects into SIM's shared Claim vocabulary without smuggling
execution state into the roadmap; canonical identities remain stable when
unordered authored collections are inserted in a different order.
