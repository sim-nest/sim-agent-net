# Reproducible federated research

Search several open or local sources without letting timing, failure, or model
influence disappear. SIM freezes authority and budgets before dispatch, keeps
partial provider evidence, fuses exact URI clusters with an auditable shared
RRF implementation, and turns only independently captured page selectors into
research evidence. Every omission stays typed and replayable.

The same product is loadable as `sim search` and projected from exact Skill
Cards through MCP. Query, fetch, research, and store-only show return one
canonical record identity across live-fake, cassette, and offline execution.
